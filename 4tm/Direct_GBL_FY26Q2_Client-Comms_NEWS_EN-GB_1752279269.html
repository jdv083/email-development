Deliver real-time refunds with Faster Refunds (Visa Direct for Card)
Customers can receive purchase refunds to their eligible Visa cards (debit, credit or pre-paid) in real-time or within minutes, instead of waiting days, so they are not left out of pocket and can manage their finances with more confidence. For our clients, this means improving operational efficiency, reducing the burden of customer queries and complaints to track refunds.

We are continuing to expand this offering across additional markets*. Please contact your Visa Direct representative to discuss availability in your market.
